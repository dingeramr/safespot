How it works

Three steps, about one minute

1

Find

Share your location or pick your area. We show the nearest SafeSpot and how many lockers are free.

2

Choose

Small, Medium or Large. Pick your times, from now or later today. You pay by the hour and see the price before you pay.

3

Pay and open

Pay with InstaPay, Apple Pay, card or wallet. Type your 6-digit code on the door. The same code opens it when you come back.

Prices

What if

We tested the awkward moments

Nobody is ever locked out of their own things. You can try each case in the booking card after you book.

I come back late

The door still opens with your code. Extra hours are added at your locker's hourly rate and you pay after you collect.

My phone is inside

You only need the code, not the phone. Choose your own code at booking so it is easy to remember.

I forgot my code

Call the number on the locker bank from any phone. We check your mobile number and ID, then give you a new code.

The door will not open

Support opens it from our side. If that fails, a technician comes within 4 hours and the rental is refunded.

Safety and security

Safe without anyone standing guard

A camera on every bank

One camera covers all 20 doors and records day and night. Footage is kept for 30 days and given to the police if needed.

A code for each booking

Your 6-digit code works for one locker and one booking, and is cancelled when you collect.

We know who booked

A mobile number for every booking, and an ID or passport number for Large lockers and full-day stays.

Cover up to EGP 10,000

Planned cover for stored items on every booking. Cash, jewellery and banned items are excluded.

Banned items

No weapons, drugs, flammable items, animals or fresh food. Bookings are logged, and suspicious use is reported.

Help on WhatsApp

Support in Arabic and English, every day from 09:00 to midnight. The number is printed on every bank.
